# Portfolio walkthrough (about 60 seconds)

1. Open the workspace. Explain the user problem: understanding a company and checking the evidence behind a brief.
2. Open the no-signup sample. Click a citation and show the source, excerpt, publication date, and historical-data label. Export the brief.
3. Sign in and research a company. Show stages starting and finishing, then the incremental report. Avoid promising a fixed response time.
4. Ask a follow-up. Refresh the page and reopen the conversation to demonstrate persisted messages and context.
5. Open Activity. Explain the extra validation call, retry bound, latency and token measurements, and why self-rated confidence was removed.
6. Show the evaluation report and ownership/recovery tests. Explain the single-worker limitation and the path to a durable queue.

Playwright records a short automated sample/evidence/export walkthrough to `frontend/test-results/*/video.webm`. The copy in `docs/walkthrough.webm`, when generated, demonstrates the no-signup UI; authentication and live cloud persistence require a separate recording after the deployment database is fixed.
